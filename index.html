About Nick

English
Nick is a dedicated physiotherapy resident with a Bachelor’s in Human Kinetics and a Master’s in Physiotherapy from the University of Ottawa. He takes an evidence-based approach to care, combining movement-focused and strength-driven strategies with manual therapy. As a former reservist in the 33 Combat Engineer Regiment, Nick brings discipline and adaptability to his practice. He has worked extensively with athletes across a variety of sports, from hockey to soccer to combat sports, and holds certifications in Level 1 Advanced Integrated Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy, Sport First Responder, and Dry Needling. A passionate advocate for active living, Nick’s training focuses on powerlifting and running, having completed two 50km ultramarathons and a 100km ultramarathon. Fluent in French, English, and Spanish, he is dedicated to delivering accessible, personalized care through Barbell Physiotherapy Inc., helping clients move better, build strength, and achieve healthier lives.
